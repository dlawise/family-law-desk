Introduction

I make this statement in support of my application for a Non-Molestation Order made without notice to the Respondent.

I shall refer to the Respondent as "Daniel" throughout this statement.

Daniel and I are associated persons pursuant to Section 62(3) of the Family Law Act 1996 because we were in an intimate personal relationship of significant duration and previously cohabited together.

I have suffered verbal abuse, emotional abuse, coercive and controlling behaviour, harassment and physical intimidation at the hands of Daniel.

Most recently, on 17 September 2026, Daniel attended outside my home without my permission, repeatedly telephoned me from withheld numbers and sent messages stating that he "would not leave me alone until I listened to him".

I fear that Daniel's behaviour is escalating and I therefore seek urgent protection by way of a Non-Molestation Order.

Background Information

I was born on 15 January 1992 and am 34 years old.

I work as an administrative officer for a logistics company.

I currently reside at 24 Meadow View Close, Birmingham, B31 4PX.

I suffer from anxiety and have received support from my GP since the breakdown of the relationship.

Daniel was born on 7 August 1989 and is 37 years old.

To the best of my knowledge, Daniel currently resides at an address in Solihull, West Midlands.

Daniel regularly abused alcohol during our relationship. His behaviour often became more aggressive when he had been drinking.

Daniel and I entered into a relationship in June 2021 and separated permanently in August 2026.

We lived together between October 2021 and August 2026.

We do not have any children together.

History of Abuse

I set out below a summary of the abuse I experienced during the relationship and after separation.

This statement has been prepared urgently to support my application and does not contain every incident that occurred during the relationship.

I seek permission to provide a further statement should additional details become necessary.

Controlling and Coercive Behaviour

Daniel's behaviour became increasingly controlling during the relationship.

He frequently demanded access to my telephone and social media accounts.

If I did not immediately respond to messages, he would accuse me of speaking to other men.

Daniel often insisted on knowing where I was, who I was with and when I would return home.

He regularly telephoned me numerous times if I was out with friends or family.

I eventually stopped seeing friends as often because it was easier than dealing with the arguments that followed.

Daniel discouraged me from spending time with my sister and frequently stated that my family were "trying to turn me against him".

I became isolated as a result of his behaviour.

Verbal and Emotional Abuse

Daniel frequently shouted at me during disagreements.

He called me names including "stupid", "pathetic" and "worthless".

He regularly told me that nobody else would want to be in a relationship with me.

He would criticise my appearance and make negative comments about my weight and clothing.

If I became upset, Daniel would mock me and state that I was "overreacting".

His behaviour caused me to lose confidence in myself and affected my self-esteem.

I constantly felt as though I was walking on eggshells around him.

Manipulative Behaviour

Daniel would often blame me for his actions.

If he behaved aggressively, he would later say that I had made him angry.

Following incidents, he would apologise and promise that things would improve.

Despite these apologies, the behaviour continued.

Whenever I attempted to discuss ending the relationship, Daniel would become emotional and claim he could not cope without me.

This made it difficult for me to leave the relationship.

Financial Control

Throughout the relationship, Daniel frequently borrowed money from me.

He rarely repaid those sums.

He expected me to cover household expenses when he had spent money on alcohol or personal purchases.

If I raised concerns about finances, Daniel accused me of being selfish.

This caused me financial and emotional stress.

Physical Intimidation

Daniel regularly used intimidating behaviour during arguments.

He would stand very close to my face whilst shouting.

On several occasions he blocked doorways to prevent me leaving rooms.

He punched walls and slammed doors during arguments.

He threw household items across rooms when angry.

Although he did not always physically assault me, I was frightened by his behaviour.

Significant Incident

Around February 2026, we had an argument after Daniel had been drinking.

During the argument he became extremely angry and accused me of being unfaithful.

He shouted loudly and backed me into a kitchen worktop.

He then grabbed my arms forcefully and prevented me from moving away.

I was frightened and crying.

After several moments he released me.

The following day he apologised and begged me not to tell anyone what had happened.

I did not report the matter to the police because I was scared of how he would react.

Post-Separation Harassment

I ended the relationship in August 2026.

Daniel refused to accept that the relationship was over.

Following the separation, he repeatedly contacted me by telephone, text message and email.

I asked him on several occasions to stop contacting me except regarding practical matters.

He ignored these requests.

He sent messages accusing me of abandoning him.

He repeatedly demanded to know where I was and who I was spending time with.

He also contacted mutual friends seeking information about me.
