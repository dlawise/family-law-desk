The Parties

Recitals

UPON the Applicant having applied for a Non-Molestation Order pursuant to Section 42 of the Family Law Act 1996.

AND UPON reading the Applicant's application and witness statement dated 22 September 2026.

AND UPON the Court being satisfied that the Applicant and Respondent are associated persons within the meaning of Section 62(3) of the Family Law Act 1996, having previously been in an intimate personal relationship of significant duration and having cohabited together.

AND UPON the Court hearing submissions on behalf of the Applicant.

AND UPON the Court finding that the Respondent has engaged in a course of conduct consisting of harassment, intimidation, coercive and controlling behaviour and unwanted contact following the breakdown of the relationship.

AND UPON the Court being satisfied that the Applicant has genuine and reasonable fears for her safety and wellbeing.

AND UPON the Court concluding that it is necessary and proportionate to grant a Non-Molestation Order for the protection of the Applicant.

IT IS ORDERED THAT
Non-Molestation Order

The Respondent, Daniel James Walker, shall not use or threaten violence against the Applicant, Sarah Elizabeth Carter.

The Respondent shall not intimidate, harass, pester, molest or interfere with the Applicant.

The Respondent shall not contact the Applicant directly or indirectly by:

a. Telephone;

b. Text message;

c. Email;

d. Social media;

e. Messaging applications;

f. Letter; or

g. Any third party.

The Respondent shall not encourage, instruct or permit any other person to contact the Applicant on his behalf.

The Respondent shall not publish, post or distribute any material relating to the Applicant on social media or by any electronic means.

The Respondent shall not attend at or within 100 metres of:

a. The Applicant's home address;

b. The Applicant's place of work; or

c. Any address where he knows or reasonably believes the Applicant is residing or staying.

The Respondent shall not keep the Applicant under surveillance or monitor her movements directly or indirectly.

The Respondent shall not communicate with the Applicant through mutual friends, acquaintances or family members.

Duration
This Order shall remain in force until 4:00pm on 24 September 2028 or further Order of the Court.
Power of Arrest
The Respondent is reminded that breach of this Order is a criminal offence pursuant to Section 42A of the Family Law Act 1996.
Service

Personal service of this Order upon the Respondent shall be effected by the Applicant.

A certificate of service shall be filed with the Court promptly after service.

Costs
There shall be no order as to costs save detailed assessment of the Applicant's publicly funded costs.
Important Notice to the Respondent

You must obey this Order.

If you disobey this Order, you may be arrested and prosecuted.

A breach of a Non-Molestation Order without reasonable excuse is a criminal offence punishable by:

Imprisonment;
A fine; or
Both.

You should read this Order carefully and seek legal advice immediately if you do not understand it.
