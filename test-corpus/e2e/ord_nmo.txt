Non-molestation Order (Family Law Act 1996 s42)
IN THE FAMILY COURT AT LEEDS
Case No. LS26F00912
BETWEEN
HANNAH REYES (Applicant)
and
DANIEL WALSH (Respondent)

Order made by District Judge Patel on 9 September 2026 at Leeds Combined Court Centre.
The Respondent did not attend. The Applicant was represented by Alex Caseworker, solicitor.
The application was made without notice to the respondent.
IT IS ORDERED:
1. The respondent must not use or threaten violence against the applicant.
2. The respondent must not intimidate, harass or pester the applicant.
3. The respondent must not contact the applicant by any means.
4. This order will last for 12 months, until 9 September 2027.
5. There will be a return date hearing on 23 September 2026 at 10:00am before a District Judge.
