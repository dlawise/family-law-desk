Recitals

UPON the Applicant having made an application for a Non-Molestation Order and Occupation Order pursuant to Part IV of the Family Law Act 1996.

AND UPON reading the Applicant's witness statement dated 12 September 2026.

AND UPON the Court being satisfied that the Applicant and Respondent are associated persons within the meaning of Section 62(3) of the Family Law Act 1996 by reason of having cohabited and having children together.

AND UPON the Court finding that there is evidence of coercive and controlling behaviour, verbal abuse, intimidation, harassment and physical violence by the Respondent towards the Applicant.

AND UPON the Court finding that the Applicant and the children have suffered harm attributable to the conduct of the Respondent.

AND UPON the Court considering Sections 33 and 42 of the Family Law Act 1996.

AND UPON the Court being satisfied that a Non-Molestation Order and Occupation Order are necessary for the protection of the Applicant and the children.

AND UPON the Court being satisfied that the balance of harm test pursuant to Section 33(7) of the Family Law Act 1996 is met.

AND UPON the Court being satisfied that if the Occupation Order were not made, the Applicant and the children are likely to suffer significant harm attributable to the conduct of the Respondent and that such harm outweighs any harm likely to be suffered by the Respondent if the Order is made.

AND UPON the Court being satisfied that the Respondent has alternative accommodation available to him.

IT IS ORDERED
Non-Molestation Order

The Respondent, Jack Thomas Harrison, shall not use or threaten violence against the Applicant, Emily Rose Turner.

The Respondent shall not intimidate, harass, pester, molest or interfere with the Applicant.

The Respondent shall not contact the Applicant directly or indirectly by:

a. Telephone;

b. Text message;

c. Email;

d. Social media;

e. Messaging applications;

f. Letter;

g. Through any third party.

The Respondent shall not encourage, permit or instruct any other person to contact the Applicant on his behalf.

The Respondent shall not publish, post or distribute material relating to the Applicant on social media or by any other electronic means.

The Respondent shall not attend at or within 100 metres of:

a. The Applicant's home address;

b. The Applicant's place of employment;

c. Any address at which he knows or reasonably believes the Applicant is residing or staying.

The Respondent shall not keep the Applicant under surveillance or monitor her movements directly or indirectly.
Child Communication Exception
For the avoidance of doubt, nothing in this Order prevents the Respondent from communicating regarding the children only through:

a. Solicitors acting for the parties; or

b. Such other written method as may subsequently be agreed between the parties in writing.

Any communication must be strictly limited to issues concerning the welfare and arrangements of the children and must not be pestering, harassing or intimidating.
Occupation Order
Exclusion from Family Home
The Respondent shall leave the family home known as:

14 Willow Grove, Sale, Greater Manchester, M33 7DR

by 6:00pm on 16 September 2026.

Having left the family home, the Respondent shall not:

a. Return to the property;

b. Enter the property;

c. Attempt to enter the property;

d. Occupy the property;

e. Remain within the property.

The Applicant shall have sole occupation of the family home.

The Respondent shall not interfere with the Applicant's peaceful occupation of the property.

Keys

The Respondent shall return all keys to the property to the Applicant or her solicitors within 24 hours of service of this Order.

Upon service of this Order, the Applicant shall be at liberty to change the locks to the property.

Collection of Personal Belongings
The Respondent may collect essential personal belongings only by:

a. Agreement between the parties' solicitors; or

b. Further Order of the Court.

Such collection shall take place in the presence of an agreed third party if required.
Duration

The Non-Molestation Order shall remain in force until 4:00pm on 15 September 2028 or further Order of the Court.

The Occupation Order shall remain in force until 4:00pm on 15 September 2027 or further Order of the Court.

Power of Arrest

The Respondent is reminded that breach of the Non-Molestation Order constitutes a criminal offence pursuant to Section 42A Family Law Act 1996.

The Applicant may report any breach immediately to the police.

Service

Personal service of this Order upon the Respondent shall be effected by the Applicant.

A Certificate of Service shall be filed with the Court within 7 days of service.

A sealed copy of this Order shall be served upon:

Northern Community Housing Association

within 7 days.

Costs
There shall be no Order as to costs save detailed assessment of the Applicant's publicly funded costs.
Important Notice to the Respondent

You must obey this Order.

If you disobey this Order you may:

Be arrested;
Be prosecuted for a criminal offence;
Be fined;
Be imprisoned.

A breach of a Non-Molestation Order without reasonable excuse is a criminal offence.
