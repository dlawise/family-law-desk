AND UPON the parties having agreed undertakings on a no findings / no admissions basis.

Respondent's Undertaking
The Respondent, Vikram Nair, undertakes to the Court that until further Order:
1. He will not use or threaten violence against the Applicant.
2. He will not intimidate, harass or pester the Applicant.
3. He will not contact the Applicant directly or indirectly except through solicitors or through agreed arrangements concerning the children.
