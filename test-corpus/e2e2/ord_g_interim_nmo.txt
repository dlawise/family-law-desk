IN THE FAMILY COURT AT NOTTINGHAM

Case Number: NG26F00601

ORDER

Family Law Act 1996

Before District Judge Cole sitting on 2 November 2026

Applicant: Tomasz Nowak

Respondent: Agnieszka Nowak

Recitals

UPON reading the Applicant's application and witness statement.

AND UPON the Court finding there is a risk of significant harm if the order is not made immediately.

AND UPON the Court being satisfied that it is just and convenient to make the order without notice.

IT IS ORDERED
The Respondent shall not:

a. Use or threaten violence against the Applicant;

b. Harass, intimidate, molest or pester the Applicant;

c. Contact the Applicant directly or indirectly.

The Respondent shall not attend within 100 metres of 12 Beech Avenue, Nottingham NG7 2PQ.

The Respondent shall not publish information relating to the Applicant.

Return Hearing
This matter shall be listed for a return hearing on the first available date after 16 November 2026 with a time estimate of 1 hour.

Service
The Applicant shall serve the Application, Witness Statement and Sealed Order upon the Respondent by personal service.

Duration
This Order shall remain in force until the return hearing or further Order.

Dated: 2 November 2026
