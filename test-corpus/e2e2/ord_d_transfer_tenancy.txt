In the Family Court at: Derby Combined Court Centre

ORDER

Family Law Act 1996

Parties
Applicant

Grace Okafor

Applicant Reference: DA/2026/0501

Respondent

Samuel Bright

Before District Judge Hale sitting in private at Derby Combined Court Centre on 8 December 2026.

The Parties

The Applicant is Grace Okafor, represented by Ms Tunde Adeyemi of counsel instructed by Dillex Solicitors.

The Respondent is Samuel Bright, appearing as a litigant in person.

RECITALS

UPON this matter coming before the Court for a FINAL HEARING.

AND UPON hearing counsel for the Applicant and the Respondent in person.

AND UPON the Court having considered the matters set out in Schedule 7 to the Family Law Act 1996.

AND UPON the Court finding that:

The Applicant remains in occupation of the property;
The Respondent has not occupied the property since 21 October 2026;
The property is the settled home of the Applicant and the child.

AND UPON the Court recording that the Respondent consented to the application.

IT IS ORDERED THAT
Transfer of Tenancy
Pursuant to Section 53 and Schedule 7 of the Family Law Act 1996, the Respondent's interest in the tenancy of:

Flat 3, 8 Mill Lane, Derby DE1 3AB

shall be transferred to the Applicant, Grace Okafor, and the tenancy shall thereafter be held solely in the Applicant's name.

Landlord Directions
Derby Homes shall amend its tenancy records accordingly and record the Applicant as the sole tenant of Flat 3, 8 Mill Lane.

Service on Landlord
A copy of this Order shall be served upon Derby Homes within 7 days of sealing.

Further Documents
The Respondent shall sign any documents reasonably required to give effect to the transfer of tenancy within 14 days of service of this Order.

Proceedings
These proceedings are concluded.

COSTS
There shall be no Order as to costs save for detailed assessment of any publicly funded party's costs.

Dated: 8 December 2026

Ordered by:

District Judge Hale
