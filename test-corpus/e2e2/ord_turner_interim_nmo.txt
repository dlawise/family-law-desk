Recitals

UPON the Applicant making an application for a Non-Molestation Order under Section 42 Family Law Act 1996.

AND UPON reading the Applicant's application and witness statement dated 12 September 2026.

AND UPON the Court being satisfied that the Applicant and Respondent are associated persons within Section 62(3) of the Family Law Act 1996 by reason of having cohabited and having children together.

AND UPON the Court considering the provisions of Section 45 Family Law Act 1996.

AND UPON the Court being satisfied that there is a risk of significant harm to the Applicant and the children attributable to the Respondent's conduct if the Order is not made immediately.

AND UPON the Court being satisfied that there are good reasons for granting the Order without notice to the Respondent.

AND UPON the Court finding that:

a. The Applicant alleges a history of coercive and controlling behaviour, verbal abuse, intimidation and physical violence;

b. The Respondent has recently forced entry into the family home and caused damage to property;

c. The Applicant fears further intimidation and harassment;

d. There is a risk that the Respondent may seek to interfere with the Applicant if notified in advance of the application.

AND UPON the Court concluding that it is just and convenient to make an interim order pending a return hearing.

IMPORTANT NOTICE TO THE RESPONDENT

This Order has been made without notice to you.

You have the right to attend the return hearing identified below and ask the Court to vary or discharge this Order.

You must obey this Order unless and until it is varied or discharged by the Court.

A breach of this Order without reasonable excuse is a criminal offence under Section 42A Family Law Act 1996 for which you may be arrested, prosecuted, fined or imprisoned.

IT IS ORDERED
Non-Molestation Order

Until further Order of the Court, the Respondent, Jack Thomas Harrison, shall not use or threaten violence against the Applicant, Emily Rose Turner.

The Respondent shall not intimidate, harass, pester, molest or interfere with the Applicant.

The Respondent shall not contact the Applicant directly or indirectly by:

a. Telephone;

b. Text message;

c. Email;

d. Social media;

e. Messaging applications;

f. Letter;

g. Any electronic means;

h. Through any third party.

The Respondent shall not encourage, permit or instruct any person to contact the Applicant on his behalf.

The Respondent shall not publish, post or distribute any material relating to the Applicant by social media or any electronic platform.

The Respondent shall not attend at or within 100 metres of:

a. The Applicant's home address;

b. The Applicant's place of work;

c. Any address where he knows or reasonably believes that the Applicant is residing or staying.

The Respondent shall not keep the Applicant under surveillance nor monitor her movements directly or indirectly.
Child Arrangements Carve-Out

For the avoidance of doubt, this Order does not prevent communication through solicitors regarding arrangements for the children.

Any communication concerning the children shall occur only through solicitors unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Return Hearing

This matter shall be listed for a return hearing on the first available date after 14 days with a time estimate of one hour.

At the return hearing the Court shall consider:

a. Whether this Order should continue;

b. Whether the application should proceed on notice;

c. Any further evidence relied upon by either party;

d. Any additional directions required.
