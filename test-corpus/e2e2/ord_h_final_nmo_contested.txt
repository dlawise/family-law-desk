IN THE FAMILY COURT AT MANCHESTER

Case Number: MA26F02990

ORDER

Family Law Act 1996

Before District Judge Hargreaves sitting in private at Manchester Civil Justice Centre on 12 January 2027

Applicant: Joanne Price

Respondent: Mark Price

The Applicant is Joanne Price, represented by Ms Tunde Adeyemi of counsel.
The Respondent is Mark Price, represented by Mr Paul Gray of counsel.

Recitals

UPON the Applicant having made an application for a Non-Molestation Order pursuant to Section 42 Family Law Act 1996.

AND UPON the Court hearing evidence from the Applicant and Respondent.

AND UPON the Court finding that the Respondent has engaged in behaviour amounting to molestation within the meaning of the Family Law Act 1996.

IT IS ORDERED

The Respondent shall not use or threaten violence against the Applicant.

The Respondent shall not intimidate, harass, pester or molest the Applicant.

The Respondent shall not contact the Applicant directly or indirectly by:

a. Telephone;

b. Text message;

c. Email;

d. Social media;

The Respondent shall not attend or attempt to attend at 5 Lime Grove, Manchester M14 5TT.

Child Communication Exception
Communication concerning the child may take place only via WhatsApp and solely for child-related matters.

Duration
This Order shall continue until 12 January 2028.

Power of Arrest
Pursuant to Section 42A Family Law Act 1996, breach of this Order constitutes a criminal offence.

Costs
No order as to costs save detailed assessment of publicly funded costs.

Dated: 12 January 2027
