DEFINITIONS
The family home is:

Flat 3, 8 Mill Lane, Derby DE1 3AB

RECITALS

This is an interim Occupation Order made against the Respondent, Samuel Bright, upon the application of the Applicant, Grace Okafor.

The Court considered:

a. The parties' housing needs and housing resources;
b. The likely effect of any Order, or of any decision not to make an Order, upon the health, safety and wellbeing of the parties and any relevant child;
c. The conduct of the parties.

Upon the Court being satisfied that it is just and reasonable to make an interim Occupation Order.

THE COURT ORDERS

OCCUPATION ORDER
Exclusion from the Family Home

1. The Respondent, Samuel Bright, shall leave the family home by 4pm on 21 October 2026.

2. Upon leaving the family home, the Respondent shall not:

a. Enter the property;
b. Attempt to enter the property;
c. Remain at the property;
d. Cause or permit any other person to seek entry on his behalf.

Exclusion Zone
3. The Respondent shall not enter within 50 metres of Flat 3, 8 Mill Lane, Derby DE1 3AB save for the purposes of attending any hearing.

Keys and Security
4. The Respondent shall immediately return all keys to the property in his possession.
5. The Applicant shall be at liberty to change the locks upon service of this Order.

DURATION OF OCCUPATION ORDER
Paragraphs 1 to 5 shall remain in force pending the next hearing.

NEXT HEARING
The application shall be listed for a return date hearing on 3 November 2026 at 10:00am before a District Judge, with a time estimate of 1 hour.

SERVICE
Personal service upon the Respondent is dispensed with, the Respondent having been present at the hearing and having understood the terms of the Order.

The Applicant shall serve a copy of this Order upon Derby Homes within 7 days.

POWER OF ARREST
A Power of Arrest shall attach to paragraphs 1 to 3 of this Order pursuant to Section 47 Family Law Act 1996.

COSTS
There shall be no Order as to costs save for detailed assessment of any publicly funded party's costs.
