IN THE FAMILY COURT AT DERBY

Case Number: DE26F00812

ORDER

Family Law Act 1996

Before District Judge Hale on 15 January 2027

Applicant: Grace Okafor

Respondent: Samuel Bright

Recitals

UPON the Court hearing evidence from the Applicant and Respondent.

AND UPON the Court considering Section 33 Family Law Act 1996.

AND UPON the Court finding that the balance of harm favours the Applicant.

IT IS ORDERED
Occupation Order
The Respondent shall not enter or attempt to enter Flat 3, 8 Mill Lane, Derby DE1 3AB.

The Respondent shall not occupy or reside at the property.

The Applicant shall have sole occupation of the property.

Additional Provisions
The Respondent may collect personal belongings only by agreement between solicitors.

Duration
The Occupation Order shall remain in force until 15 July 2027.

Costs
No order as to costs save detailed assessment of publicly funded costs.

Dated: 15 January 2027
