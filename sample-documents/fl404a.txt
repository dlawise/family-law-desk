Family Law Act 1996
Non-molestation Order
FL404a
In the Family Court sitting at Birmingham Civil and Family Justice Centre
Case Number: BM26F00231
The Applicant: Fatima Khan
The Respondent: Imran Khan
Date of Birth of Respondent: 04/05/1988

Before District Judge Okafor sitting at Birmingham Civil and Family Justice Centre on 9 September 2026.

The court read the statement of the applicant. The respondent was not present and not represented. The order was made without notice to the respondent because of the risk of significant harm to the applicant and the children.

IT IS ORDERED THAT:
1. The respondent (Imran Khan) is forbidden to use or threaten violence against the applicant (Fatima Khan).
2. The respondent is forbidden to intimidate, harass or pester the applicant.
3. The respondent is forbidden to contact the applicant, whether directly or indirectly, except through solicitors.
4. The respondent is forbidden to go within 50 metres of 24 Park Lane, Birmingham B13 8XX.
5. This order shall remain in force until 5 October 2026 at 4pm when it will be reviewed.
6. There shall be a further hearing on 5 October 2026 at 2pm at Birmingham Civil and Family Justice Centre, time estimate 1 hour, for the court to consider whether the order should continue (the return date).
7. The respondent may apply to the court on 48 hours' notice to vary or discharge this order.
8. Service of this order on the respondent shall be effected personally by the applicant's solicitors.

PENAL NOTICE
If you, Imran Khan, disobey this order you may be held to be in contempt of court and may be imprisoned, fined or have your assets seized. Disobeying this order is a criminal offence under section 42A of the Family Law Act 1996.
