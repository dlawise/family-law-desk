Children Act 1989
Child Arrangements Order
C43
In the Family Court at Birmingham
Case No: BM26P00455
Applicant: Fatima Khan
Respondent: Imran Khan
The children: Zainab Khan (born 12 March 2019), Yusuf Khan (born 9 June 2022)

Before Her Honour Judge Marsh on 12 October 2026.

Final hearing. Both parties attended with counsel.

THE COURT ORDERS:
1. The children shall live with the Applicant mother.
2. The children shall spend time with the Respondent father every second Saturday from 10am to 4pm, supervised by a contact centre approved by the court, for a period of 6 months.
3. The Respondent father must not remove the children from the jurisdiction of England and Wales without the written consent of the Applicant mother or an order of the court. This is a prohibited steps order.
4. There shall be no order as to costs.
5. The case is now concluded and the file closed.
